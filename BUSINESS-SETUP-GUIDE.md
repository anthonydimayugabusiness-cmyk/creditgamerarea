# Credit Gamer Area - Business Setup Guide

## 🏢 BUSINESS STRUCTURE OPTIONS

### Option 1: Sole Proprietorship (Start Here)
**Best for:** Getting started quickly, testing the model
- **Pros:** No formation costs, simple taxes, full control
- **Cons:** Personal liability, harder to get business credit
- **Setup:** Just start operating - use your SSN for taxes

### Option 2: LLC (Recommended for Scale)
**Best for:** Long-term growth, liability protection
- **Pros:** Personal asset protection, business credit building, professional image
- **Cons:** $50-$500 formation cost (varies by state), annual fees
- **Setup:** File with your state's Secretary of State

### Option 3: S-Corp (Later Stage)
**Best for:** High revenue ($50K+/year), tax optimization
- **Pros:** Tax savings on self-employment tax
- **Cons:** Complex setup, payroll requirements
- **Setup:** Form LLC first, then elect S-Corp status with IRS

**Purpose:** Display ads on your website
- **Sign up:** https://www.google.com/adsense
- **Requirements:** Quality website, original content, traffic

## 📊 TRAFFIC BUYING STRATEGY

### Phase 1: Testing ($500-1,000 budget)
- Test 3-4 traffic sources
- $10-20/day per source
- Track cost per visitor
- Identify best performing channels

### Phase 2: Scaling ($2,000-5,000/month)
- Double down on winning channels
- Optimize ad creative and targeting
- A/B test landing pages

### Phase 3: Optimization ($5,000+/month)
- Advanced retargeting
- Lookalike audiences
- Content promotion

### Key Metrics to Track:
- **CPV (Cost Per Visitor):** Target under $0.50
- **RPM (Revenue Per 1,000 views):** Target $10+
- **Break-even:** CPV < RPM/1000
- **Example:** If RPM is $15, you can pay up to $0.015 per visitor
