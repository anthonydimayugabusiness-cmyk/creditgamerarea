10 Best Finance Instagram Accounts for Working Moms

Quick money tips between meetings and school pickup

I don't have time for long YouTube videos or blog posts during the work week. But I do scroll Instagram while waiting in the carpool line. These accounts deliver real financial advice in 30-second Reels and carousel posts.

💰 Budgeting & Saving

1. Tori Dunlap (Her First $100K)

@herfirst100k
2.1M followers
Saving strategies & investing basics

Tori breaks down complex topics into pink-themed carousel posts that actually make sense. Her "Financial Foundation" series taught me more about Roth IRAs than my finance degree. She focuses on helping women build wealth without guilt.

Best content: "5 Money Myths Holding You Back" and "How to Negotiate a Raise" Reels

Follow on Instagram →

2. The Financial Diet

@thefinancialdiet
850K followers
Career & lifestyle finance

Chelsea and team post about the intersection of money, career, and life. Their "Money Diaries" series (real people's spending) is addictive and eye-opening. Great for working moms navigating salary negotiations and childcare costs.

Best content: "A Week of Spending" stories and career advice carousels

Follow on Instagram →

3. Kumiko Love (The Budget Mom)

@thebudgetmom
520K followers
Cash envelope budgeting

Kumiko's colorful budget printables and cash envelope system are everywhere for a reason - they work. She posts daily motivation, budget check-ins, and real talk about overspending. Very relatable for moms who struggle with impulse purchases.

Best content: Budget template freebies and "Paycheck Budget" breakdowns

Follow on Instagram →

🏠 Family Finance & Frugal Living

4. Jordan Page (FunCheapOrFree)

@fun cheap or free
580K followers
Family budgeting & meal planning

Jordan has 8 kids and feeds them on a budget that seems impossible until she shows you how. Her "Shelf Cooking" method and bulk cooking Reels have saved my family hundreds. She proves you can have a big family and still save money.

Best content: Meal prep videos and "Feeding my family for $X" posts

Follow on Instagram →

5. One Big Happy Life

@onebighappylife
180K followers
Debt payoff & family money

Joseph and Tasha share their journey paying off $250K+ while raising kids. Their content focuses on budgeting as a family, teaching kids about money, and staying motivated during long debt payoff journeys.

Best content: Family budget meetings and "What we spent this month" transparency

Follow on Instagram →

6. Canna Campbell (SugarMammaTV)

@sugarmamma.tv
95K followers
Australian frugal living

Australian financial advisor who focuses on mindful spending and "financial mindfulness." Her "\$1,000 Project" (saving in small chunks) is perfect for moms who feel overwhelmed by big savings goals.

Best content: "\$1,000 Project" updates and mindful spending tips

Follow on Instagram →

8. Delyanne The Money Coach

Investing for beginners

Delyanne went from $150K in debt to financially independent. She focuses on index fund investing and FIRE (Financial Independence Retire Early) for regular people. Her "Investing 101" highlight is essential viewing.

Minimalism saves money

Dawn shows how owning less naturally leads to spending less. Her decluttering motivation posts and "sell your stuff" tips helped me make $800 selling things I didn't need. Perfect for overwhelmed moms drowning in stuff.

10. Aja Dang

Aja documented paying off $200K in debt in real-time. Her Instagram shows the messy reality of budgeting - overspending, setbacks, and wins. Very relatable and motivating.

Follow on Instagram →

How I Use Instagram for Finance (Without Getting Overwhelmed)

I follow all 10, but I don't consume everything. Here's my system:

  • Morning scroll (5 min): Check The Budget Mom and Her First $100K for daily motivation
  • Lunch break (10 min): Watch Mrs. Dow Jones Reels for investing education
  • Evening (5 min): Check One Big Happy Life for family money inspiration
  • Weekend: Save carousel posts to review later when I have time to implement

Pro tip: Create an Instagram Collection called "Money Tips" and save posts you want to revisit. I review mine monthly and implement one strategy.
