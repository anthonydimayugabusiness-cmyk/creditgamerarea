15 Best Finance YouTubers to Watch in 2026

From beginners to advanced investors - find your money mentor

I learned more about money from YouTube than from my entire formal education. These creators break down complex topics into binge-worthy content that actually makes sense. Here are the best finance YouTubers for every stage of your money journey.

🌟 Best for Beginners

1. Graham Stephan

@GrahamStephan
5.2M subscribers
Real estate & frugal living

Graham made his first million by 26 through real estate and now shares exactly how he did it. His "How I'd Start Over" series is pure gold for beginners. He reviews celebrity homes, reacts to spending habits, and breaks down how to save 50%+ of your income.

Start with: "How I'd Invest $1,000" or "Millionaire Reacts to Normal People's Spending"

Watch on YouTube →

2. The Plain Bagel

@ThePlainBagel
1.1M subscribers
No-BS investing education

Richard Coffin is a former investment analyst who explains investing without the hype. No "get rich quick" nonsense - just solid, research-backed education. His "Stock Market for Beginners" playlist should be required viewing.

Start with: "How to Start Investing" or "Why Most People Lose Money in Stocks"

Watch on YouTube →

3. Two Cents (PBS Digital Studios)

@TwoCentsPBS
1.4M subscribers
Behavioral economics & psychology

Hosts Philip Olson and Julia Lorenz-Olson (both CFPs) explore the psychology behind money decisions. Why do we overspend? Why is saving so hard? They make behavioral economics actually fun.

Start with: "The Psychology of Money" or "Why You Buy Things You Don't Need"

Watch on YouTube →

💼 Best for Working Professionals

4. The Financial Diet

@TheFinancialDiet
1.3M subscribers
Women & career-focused content

Chelsea Fagan built this channel specifically for women navigating money and career. Topics include salary negotiation, side hustles for working moms, and building wealth while dealing with the gender pay gap.

Start with: "How to Negotiate Your Salary" or "A Realistic Money Diary"

Watch on YouTube →

5. One Big Happy Life

@OneBigHappyLife
580K subscribers
Working moms & families

Joseph and Tasha share their journey paying off $250K+ in debt while raising kids. Real talk about the challenges of budgeting with children, meal planning, and building wealth as a family.

Start with: "How We Paid Off $250K" or "Budgeting With Kids"

Watch on YouTube →

6. Ryan Scribner

@RyanScribner
850K subscribers
Side hustles & passive income

Ryan documents his journey from $0 to $1M net worth through dividend investing and side hustles. Great for anyone wanting to build multiple income streams alongside their 9-5.

Start with: "How to Build Multiple Income Streams" or "Dividend Investing for Beginners"

Watch on YouTube →

@AndreiJikh

@JosephCarlson
420K subscribers

Joseph focuses on dividend growth investing - buying companies that consistently increase their dividends. He analyzes individual stocks and explains his reasoning in detail.

Start with: "My Dividend Portfolio" or "How to Pick Dividend Stocks"

Watch on YouTube →

2M subscribers

Kevin is a real estate millionaire who shares everything - his deals, his mistakes, market predictions. He also covers stocks and macro economics. High energy, lots of content.

Start with: "How to Buy Your First Rental Property" or his market update videos

Watch on YouTube →

@HumphreyYang
1.2M subscribers

15. Nate O'Brien

@NateOBrien

Minimalism & intentional living

Nate combines minimalism with personal finance. His aesthetic videos about simple living, intentional spending, and building wealth through discipline are both calming and motivating.

Watch on YouTube →

How to Actually Learn From YouTube

Watching finance YouTube is entertainment. Taking action is education. Here's my system:

  1. Pick 2-3 creators max - Don't subscribe to all 15. Pick the ones that match your goals.
  2. Take notes - Actually write down action items while watching.
  3. Implement one thing - After each video, do ONE thing they recommended.
  4. Track your progress - Measure net worth monthly, not weekly.
