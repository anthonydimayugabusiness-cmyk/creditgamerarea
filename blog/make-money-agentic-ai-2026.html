What Is Agentic AI?

Agentic AI refers to autonomous systems that can:

  • Work independently — no constant prompting needed
  • Use tools — code, APIs, browsers, file systems
  • Make decisions — prioritize tasks, handle errors, adapt
  • Scale horizontally — spin up sub-agents for parallel work

Think of it as hiring an employee who never sleeps, works for pennies per hour, and can clone themselves when workload increases. That's the power of platforms like OpenClaw.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Trying to do everything at once — Pick one method. Master it. Then expand.
  • Undercharging — AI doesn't mean cheap. Charge for value, not hours.
  • Ignoring the human element — Clients buy from humans. Be the face; let AI be the engine.
  • Over-automating too soon — Start manual, then automate what works.
  • Not documenting processes — If you can't document it, you can't scale it.
