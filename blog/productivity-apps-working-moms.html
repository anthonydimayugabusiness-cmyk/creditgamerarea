7 Best Productivity Apps for Working Moms to Reclaim Their Time

Working moms juggle it all. These 7 productivity apps help you reclaim 5+ hours per week—so you have more time for what actually matters.

February 25, 2026 • 10 min read

Between the morning rush, work deadlines, school pickups, dinner prep, and bedtime routines, working moms are operating at maximum capacity. There's always one more thing that needs doing—and never enough time.

But here's what I discovered after burning out hard: it's not about doing more. It's about doing things smarter. The right productivity apps don't add to your workload—they remove friction from your day.

"I gained back 7 hours per week by automating the mental load I didn't even realize I was carrying."

These are the apps that actually moved the needle for me—and for dozens of other working moms I interviewed.

1. Todoist - The Brain Dump Solution

✅ Todoist Best Overall

Free plan available Pro: $4/month

Your brain wasn't designed to remember grocery lists, work deadlines, permission slips, and dentist appointments simultaneously. Todoist becomes your external brain.

Why working moms love it:

  • Natural language input ("Pick up dry cleaning Friday at 5pm")
  • Recurring tasks for weekly routines
  • Shared projects with your partner
  • Integrates with Alexa, Google Assistant, Slack
⏱️ Time saved: 2+ hours/week by eliminating mental load and forgotten tasks

2. Cozi - The Family Command Center

📅 Cozi Best for Families

Free plan available Gold: $29.99/year

One shared family calendar that everyone can actually see. Color-coded by person. Accessible on any device. Game-changing.

Key features:

  • Shared shopping lists (no more "did you get milk?")
  • Meal planning with recipe storage
  • Appointment reminders for the whole family
  • Works even if your partner refuses to download apps (email notifications)
⏱️ Time saved: 1.5 hours/week by eliminating coordination texts and double-checking

3. Toggl Track - Find Your Time Leaks

⏱️ Toggl Track Best for Awareness

Free for basic use Starter: $9/month

Track where your time actually goes for one week. The results will shock you. (I discovered I was spending 8 hours per week on Instagram without realizing it.)

The wake-up call: Once you see the data, you can't unsee it. You'll naturally start making better choices about how you spend your hours.

⏱️ Time saved: 3+ hours/week by eliminating unconscious time-wasters

4. Notion - The Second Brain

🧠 Notion Most Powerful

Free personal use Plus: $8/month

Notes, databases, calendars, wikis—all in one place. It has a learning curve, but once set up, it becomes your digital life headquarters.

Working mom templates:

  • Kids' medical records and vaccination schedules
  • Meal planning database with grocery lists
  • Home maintenance tracker
  • Travel planning hub
⏱️ Time saved: 2 hours/week by having everything searchable and organized

5. Forest - Beat Phone Addiction

🌲 Forest Best for Focus

$3.99 one-time

Plant a virtual tree. If you leave the app, the tree dies. It sounds silly, but it's weirdly effective for staying off your phone during focused work or family time.

Use it for:

  • Focused work sessions
  • Quality time with kids (no phone distractions)
  • Bedtime wind-down (no scrolling at night)
⏱️ Time saved: 5+ hours/week by reducing mindless phone use

6. Instacart/Amazon Subscribe & Save - Automate the Errands

🛒 Grocery Delivery Best Time-Saver

Delivery fees vary

Yes, there's a fee. But calculate your hourly rate. If you earn $30/hour and grocery shopping takes 2 hours, that's $60 of your time. A $10 delivery fee is a bargain.

Pro tip: Set up recurring orders for staples you buy every week. It takes 5 minutes to set up and saves hours long-term.

⏱️ Time saved: 2-3 hours/week by eliminating grocery runs

7. Calendly - End the Scheduling Ping-Pong

📆 Calendly Best for Scheduling

Free basic plan Standard: $10/month

Send someone a link. They pick a time that works. No more "How about Tuesday? No, Wednesday? Wait, I'm busy then..."

Working mom magic: Set your availability around school drop-off, pickup, and other non-negotiables. People can only book when you're actually free.

⏱️ Time saved: 1 hour/week by eliminating scheduling back-and-forth

The Real Talk

Here's the truth: apps alone won't fix an overloaded schedule. But they can remove the friction that makes every day feel harder than it needs to be.

Start with just one. The one that solves your biggest pain point right now. Master it. Then add another if you need it.

The goal isn't to become a productivity robot. It's to create enough space in your life that you can actually breathe—and enjoy the moments that matter.

Your Challenge

Pick one app from this list. Download it today. Spend 15 minutes setting it up. Use it for one week.

Notice what changes. Notice what feels easier. That's the beginning of reclaiming your time.
