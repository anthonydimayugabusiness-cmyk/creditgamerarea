Hawaii for (Almost) Free: The Ultimate Miles & Points Guide

How to fly to paradise and stay in luxury hotels using credit card points, airline miles, and transfer partners — saving thousands on your dream Hawaiian vacation

📅 Feb 23, 2026 ⏱️ 15 min read ✈️ Travel Rewards Deep Dive

Picture this: You're sipping a mai tai on Waikiki Beach, watching the sunset paint the Pacific in shades of orange and pink. The best part? You got here for less than the cost of a fancy dinner back home.

This isn't a fantasy. With the right credit card strategy and knowledge of transfer partners, you can fly to Hawaii and stay in incredible hotels for a fraction of the retail price — sometimes for just taxes and fees.

In this comprehensive guide, we're breaking down everything you need to know: the best routes from the mainland, which transfer partners offer the sweetest deals, how to book Hawaiian Airlines, where to stay using points, and how to hop between islands without breaking the bank.

🌺 The Bottom Line Up Front

With strategic credit card sign-up bonuses and transfer partners, you can book round-trip flights to Hawaii for as little as 25,000 miles and luxury hotel stays for 40,000-60,000 points per night. A family of four can save $5,000+ on a Hawaiian vacation.

Why Hawaii Is the Perfect Miles & Points Destination

Hawaii represents one of the best values in the travel rewards world for several reasons:

  • Predictable pricing: Unlike international destinations with seasonal fluctuations, Hawaii award availability is relatively consistent year-round
  • Multiple airline options: Nearly every major U.S. carrier flies to Hawaii, creating competition and award availability
  • Sweet spot redemptions: Several transfer partners offer surprisingly low mileage requirements
  • Hotel variety: From budget-friendly to ultra-luxury, all major hotel chains have properties across the islands
  • Inter-island travel: Short, cheap flights between islands make multi-island trips feasible

The average family of four spends $8,000-$12,000 on a week-long Hawaiian vacation. With the strategies in this guide, you can cut that in half — or more.

Best Routes from the Mainland to Hawaii

Your departure city matters. Some routes offer better award availability, lower taxes, and more comfortable aircraft. Here's what you need to know:

West Coast Gateways (Best Value)

If you live on the West Coast, you're already winning. These cities offer the shortest flights, most award availability, and lowest mileage requirements:

  • Los Angeles (LAX): Direct flights to all major Hawaiian islands on Hawaiian, United, American, Delta, Alaska, and Southwest
  • San Francisco (SFO): United's hub with excellent award availability; Hawaiian and Alaska also strong options
  • Seattle (SEA): Alaska Airlines hub with competitive award pricing
  • San Diego (SAN): Growing number of direct options, especially on Alaska and Hawaiian
  • Portland (PDX): Alaska Airlines stronghold with good availability

💡 Pro Tip: Positioning Flights

If you're not on the West Coast, consider booking a separate positioning flight to LAX or SFO. A cheap domestic ticket ($100-200) can unlock significantly better Hawaii award options. Just leave at least 3 hours between flights.

Central and East Coast Options

Living east of the Rockies? You have options too, though you'll need more miles:

  • Denver (DEN): United hub with direct flights to Honolulu
  • Dallas (DFW): American Airlines hub with nonstop service
  • Chicago (ORD): United and American both offer direct flights
  • New York (JFK/EWR): United, Hawaiian, and JetBlue fly direct (premium pricing)
  • Atlanta (ATL): Delta hub with solid award availability

The Secret Weapons: Transfer Partners That Save You Thousands

Here's where the magic happens. While you could book directly through airline websites using miles, the real sweet spots come from transferring credit card points to airline partners. These two programs are game-changers for Hawaii:

🇸🇬 Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer

The Sweet Spot: Fly United to Hawaii for just 25,000 miles round-trip in economy (vs. 45,000+ booking through United directly).

Why It Works: Singapore Airlines is a Star Alliance partner with United, but their award chart for Hawaii flights is significantly cheaper. United charges 45,000 miles for mainland-to-Hawaii round-trip, but Singapore only charges 25,000 for the exact same United flights.

Transfer Partners: Chase Ultimate Rewards, American Express Membership Rewards, Citi ThankYou Points, Capital One Miles

How to Book:

  1. Search for United saver award availability on United.com (look for flights priced at 22,500 miles one-way)
  2. Create a free Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer account
  3. Transfer points from your credit card to KrisFlyer (usually instant)
  4. Call Singapore Airlines to book: 1-800-742-3333 (they can't book Hawaii awards online)
  5. Pay just 25,000 miles + ~$11 in taxes

Important Notes:

  • Must book round-trip (no one-way awards at this price)
  • Limited to United saver availability
  • Changes cost $25; cancellations cost $75
  • Points expire after 36 months of inactivity

🇬🇧 British Airways Avios

The Sweet Spot: Fly American Airlines or Alaska Airlines to Hawaii for as little as 26,000 Avios round-trip from the West Coast.

Why It Works: British Airways uses a distance-based award chart. The West Coast to Hawaii is just under 3,000 miles, putting it in the sweet spot of 13,000 Avios each way. From farther cities, it's still often cheaper than booking through American or Alaska directly.

Transfer Partners: Chase Ultimate Rewards, American Express Membership Rewards, Capital One Miles, Bilt Rewards

Sample Redemptions:

  • Los Angeles to Honolulu: 13,000 Avios each way (26,000 round-trip)
  • Phoenix to Honolulu: 16,250 Avios each way (32,500 round-trip)
  • Dallas to Honolulu: 20,000 Avios each way (40,000 round-trip)

How to Book:

  1. Search for American Airlines or Alaska Airlines award availability on aa.com or alaskaair.com
  2. Create a free British Airways Executive Club account
  3. Transfer points to Avios (usually instant)
  4. Book online at britishairways.com or call 1-800-452-1201

Important Notes:

  • Can book one-way or round-trip
  • No close-in booking fees
  • Cancellation fees are low (usually $55 or less)
  • Avios expire after 36 months of inactivity

🚨 Hidden Gem: Turkish Miles&Smiles

Turkish Airlines charges just 15,000 miles round-trip to Hawaii on United flights — the absolute cheapest option. However, booking requires calling their customer service (often a lengthy process) and availability can be limited. Best for experienced award bookers with patience.

Booking Hawaiian Airlines Directly

Sometimes you want to fly the airline of Hawaii itself. Hawaiian Airlines offers a unique product with island-inspired service, complimentary meals, and that authentic aloha spirit. Here's how to book them with points:

Hawaiian Airlines Award Chart

Hawaiian uses a zone-based award chart with the following mainland pricing:

  • West Coast to Hawaii: 40,000 miles round-trip (economy), 80,000 (business)
  • JFK/Boston to Hawaii: 60,000 miles round-trip (economy), 120,000 (business)
  • Other cities: 50,000-60,000 miles round-trip (economy)

How to Earn Hawaiian Miles

  • Hawaiian Airlines World Elite Mastercard: Sign-up bonus typically 60,000-70,000 miles
  • Transfer from Amex: 1:1 ratio (sometimes with bonuses)
  • Transfer from Marriott: 3:1 ratio (not recommended unless topping off)
  • Shopping portal: Hawaiian Airlines has a shopping portal for bonus miles

When to Book Hawaiian Direct

Despite higher mileage costs, booking Hawaiian Airlines directly makes sense when:

  • You want the authentic Hawaiian experience from takeoff
  • United/AA saver availability is nonexistent for your dates
  • You're flying from cities Hawaiian serves nonstop (JFK, Boston, Austin, Orlando)
  • You have Amex points to transfer during a bonus promotion
  • You want to use the "Cash + Miles" option for partial redemptions

Hotel Redemptions: Where to Stay for Free

Now that you've got your flights sorted, let's talk about where to lay your head. Hawaii has options for every points budget, from budget-friendly to ultra-luxury.

Hilton Honors

Hilton has an incredible presence in Hawaii, ranging from 40,000 to 95,000 points per night. The best part? Fifth night free on award stays — effectively giving you a 20% discount on 5+ night stays.

🏨 Top Hilton Properties in Hawaii

Hilton Hawaiian Village (Oahu) 60,000 pts/night
Grand Wailea (Maui) — Waldorf Astoria 95,000 pts/night
Hilton Waikoloa Village (Big Island) 70,000 pts/night
DoubleTree Alana Waikiki (Oahu) 50,000 pts/night

How to Earn Hilton Points:

  • Hilton Honors American Express cards: Sign-up bonuses of 100,000-175,000 points
  • Transfer from Amex: 1:2 ratio (1 Amex point = 2 Hilton points)
  • Paid stays: 10x+ points per dollar as a member

Marriott Bonvoy

Marriott has the largest footprint in Hawaii, with properties across all islands. Their dynamic pricing means rates vary, but here are typical ranges:

🏨 Top Marriott Properties in Hawaii

Sheraton Waikiki (Oahu) 50,000-70,000 pts/night
Wailea Beach Resort (Maui) 60,000-85,000 pts/night
Marriott Waikiki (Oahu) 40,000-60,000 pts/night
Westin Hapuna Beach (Big Island) 50,000-70,000 pts/night

How to Earn Marriott Points:

  • Marriott Bonvoy credit cards: Sign-up bonuses of 75,000-150,000 points
  • Transfer from Chase/Amex: 1:1 ratio (not usually the best value)
  • Fifth night free: On award stays of 5+ nights

Hyatt

Hyatt has a smaller but exceptional Hawaii portfolio. Their award chart is fixed (no dynamic pricing), making planning easier:

🏨 Hyatt Properties in Hawaii

Andaz Maui at Wailea 40,000 pts/night
Grand Hyatt Kauai 35,000 pts/night
Hyatt Regency Waikiki (Oahu) 25,000 pts/night
Hyatt Place Waikiki (Oahu) 15,000 pts/night

How to Earn Hyatt Points:

  • Chase Ultimate Rewards: Transfer 1:1 (best value for Hyatt)
  • World of Hyatt credit card: Sign-up bonus of 60,000+ points
  • Bilt Rewards: Transfer 1:1 (great for rent earners)

🏆 Best Value Alert: Hyatt

Hyatt consistently offers the best value for Hawaii redemptions. The Andaz Maui — a $1,200+/night hotel — costs just 40,000 points. That's 3+ cents per point in value, far exceeding typical redemption rates.

IHG One Rewards

IHG has several solid options, particularly on Oahu:

  • InterContinental Waikiki: 60,000-70,000 points/night
  • Holiday Inn Express Waikiki: 30,000-40,000 points/night (includes breakfast)
  • Hotel Indigo Honolulu: 35,000-45,000 points/night

Island Hopping on Points

No Hawaiian vacation is complete without visiting multiple islands. Here's how to get between them:

Hawaiian Airlines Inter-Island

The most comprehensive network with flights between all major islands:

  • Cost: 7,500 Hawaiian miles each way
  • Cash price: $100-200 typically
  • Best value: Use cash for short hops, save miles for mainland flights

Southwest Airlines

A game-changer for inter-island travel:

  • Cost: 4,000-8,000 Rapid Rewards points each way
  • Perks: Two free checked bags, no change fees
  • Transfer from: Chase Ultimate Rewards 1:1

Mokulele Airlines

For smaller airports and unique routes:

  • Best for: Hana (Maui), Lanai, Molokai
  • Book with: Cash (typically $80-150)

Putting It All Together: Sample Itineraries

Budget Backpacker (7 Days)

💰 Total Points Needed: ~100,000

  1. Flights: 25,000 Singapore miles (United) round-trip from West Coast
  2. Hotel: 75,000 Hyatt points for 5 nights at Hyatt Place Waikiki (15,000/night)
  3. Inter-island: $100 cash for round-trip to Maui on Southwest
  4. Total cash outlay: ~$200 (taxes, inter-island flights, resort fees)

Family of Four (7 Days)

💰 Total Points Needed: ~400,000

  1. Flights: 100,000 Singapore miles (4 × 25,000) for family
  2. Hotel: 240,000 Hilton points for 6 nights at Hilton Hawaiian Village (40,000/night, 5th night free)
  3. Inter-island: 32,000 Southwest points for family day trip to Big Island
  4. Total cash outlay: ~$500 (taxes, fees, some meals)
  5. Savings vs. cash: $6,000+

Luxury Escape (10 Days)

💰 Total Points Needed: ~300,000

  1. Flights: 26,000 British Airways Avios round-trip from LAX
  2. Maui (5 nights): 200,000 Hyatt points at Andaz Maui (40,000/night)
  3. Kauai (4 nights): 140,000 Marriott points at Grand Hyatt Kauai (35,000/night)
  4. Inter-island: 15,000 Hawaiian miles
  5. Total cash outlay: ~$800 (taxes, resort fees, some dining)
  6. Savings vs. cash: $8,000+

Credit Card Strategy for Hawaii

Ready to start earning? Here's your card application strategy:

First Cards (Beginners)

  • Chase Sapphire Preferred: 60,000 points sign-up bonus — transfers to Singapore, British Airways, Hyatt, Southwest
  • Chase Freedom Flex: No annual fee, 5x rotating categories to complement Sapphire

Next Level (After 48 Months)

  • Chase Sapphire Reserve: 60,000 points, 3x on travel/dining, $300 travel credit
  • American Express Gold: 60,000+ points, 4x on dining/groceries
  • Capital One Venture X: 75,000 miles, transfers to Singapore, Turkish, Air France

Hotel Cards

  • World of Hyatt: 60,000 points — best value for Hawaii hotels
  • Hilton Honors Surpass: 130,000+ points — great for Hilton properties
  • Marriott Bonvoy Boundless: 100,000+ points — largest hotel footprint

🎯 The 5/24 Rule Warning

Chase has a strict rule: if you've opened 5+ credit cards in the past 24 months from ANY bank, they won't approve you for most of their cards. Get your Chase cards FIRST before moving to Amex, Capital One, or Citi.

Tips for Booking Success

  • Book 11 months out: Award availability opens ~330 days in advance
  • Be flexible: Tuesday/Wednesday departures often have better availability
  • Set alerts: Use ExpertFlyer or Seats.aero to track award space
  • Check frequently: Award space changes daily; persistence pays off
  • Have backup dates: Don't get locked into specific days
  • Book one-ways: Mix and match programs for outbound/return
  • Consider positioning: Fly to LAX/SFO cheaply, then use miles to Hawaii

The Bottom Line

Hawaii doesn't have to be a once-in-a-lifetime splurge. With the right credit card strategy and knowledge of transfer partners, you can visit the islands regularly for a fraction of the retail cost.

The key takeaways:

  • Singapore Airlines: 25,000 miles round-trip on United (best value)
  • British Airways: 26,000+ Avios from West Coast on American/Alaska
  • Hyatt: Best hotel value, especially Andaz Maui at 40,000 points
  • Southwest: Great for inter-island hops with free bags
  • Start with Chase: Get Sapphire Preferred and Freedom cards first

A family of four can easily save $5,000-$8,000 on a Hawaiian vacation using these strategies. That's money you can spend on luaus, helicopter tours, or your next adventure.

Mahalo for reading, and happy travels! 🌺
