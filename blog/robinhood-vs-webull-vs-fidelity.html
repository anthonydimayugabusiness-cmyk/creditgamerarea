Choosing your first investing app is a big decision. The platform you pick will shape how you learn, what you can invest in, and how much you'll pay in fees. Get it wrong, and you might pay hidden costs or miss out on features you need later.

I've used all three platforms extensively. In this guide, I'll break down Robinhood, Webull, and Fidelity — comparing fees, features, investment options, and who each platform is best for. By the end, you'll know exactly which one fits your investing style.

⚡ Quick Verdict

Choose Robinhood if: You want the simplest, most beginner-friendly experience
Choose Webull if: You want more research tools and don't mind complexity
Choose Fidelity if: You want the most investment options and best customer service

Which One Should YOU Choose?

Choose Robinhood If... ✅

  • You're a complete beginner who feels overwhelmed by investing
  • You want the simplest possible experience
  • You want to buy fractional shares of expensive stocks (Amazon, Tesla)
  • You want to trade crypto alongside stocks
  • You're comfortable with basic features and no hand-holding

Best for: First-time investors, people who want to dip their toes in with small amounts, crypto-curious investors.

Choose Webull If... ✅

  • You want more research tools than Robinhood offers
  • You want to practice trading with paper money first
  • You plan to trade during extended hours
  • You want IRA options for retirement savings
  • You're willing to learn a slightly more complex interface

Best for: Intermediate beginners who want room to grow, active traders, people who want to practice before using real money.

Choose Fidelity If... ✅

  • You want access to every investment type (stocks, bonds, mutual funds, CDs)
  • Retirement investing is a priority
  • You value customer service and want phone support
  • You want the best research and educational resources
  • You're investing for the long term, not trading

Best for: Serious long-term investors, retirement savers, people who want the full-service experience.

My Recommendation for Beginners

If you're truly just starting out, here's my advice:

  1. Start with Fidelity for your retirement accounts (IRA). The tax advantages are too good to pass up, and Fidelity has the best retirement tools.
  2. Open a Robinhood account for fun/play money. Use it to learn with small amounts ($50-100). The simple interface removes intimidation.
  3. Move to Webull or stay with Fidelity once you outgrow Robinhood's limited features.

🎯 The Bottom Line

All three are legitimate, safe, and commission-free. You can't really make a "wrong" choice — but you can choose one that doesn't fit your needs. Start simple (Robinhood), then upgrade as you learn. The most important thing is starting, not having the perfect platform.
