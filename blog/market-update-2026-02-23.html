Market Update: Bitcoin Dips Below $65K, Big Tech in Bear Territory, Tariff Drama Continues

📅 February 23, 2026 • ⏱️ 6 min read

What's good, money gamers? This week in the markets felt like a ranked match where your entire team decided to try off-meta picks. We've got Bitcoin getting dunked on, Big Tech officially entering bear market territory, and enough tariff drama to fill a whole season of a political Netflix series. Let's break it down.

📉 The Market Snapshot

After a brief moment of hope on Friday (thanks to a Supreme Court ruling we'll get to), the markets are looking shaky again. The S&P 500 closed Friday at 6,909.51 — up 0.69% for the day, but still nursing wounds from a brutal few weeks. The Nasdaq managed a 0.9% gain to end at 22,886.07, finally snapping a five-week losing streak (its longest since May 2022).

But don't celebrate too hard. Futures are pointing down again as I write this, and the Vibe Check™ on Wall Street is decidedly "anxious."

🐻 Big Tech Enters the Bear Zone

Here's the tea: Amazon and Microsoft are now officially in bear markets — down more than 20% from their recent peaks. And no, it's not because they missed earnings. Both actually beat expectations:

  • Amazon (AMZN): Down ~23% from its 52-week high, trading around $199. RSI at 23 (deeply oversold territory)
  • Microsoft (MSFT): Down ~27% from its peak at $553, now around $401. RSI at 32 (also oversold)

So why the selloff? One word: capex. These companies are spending BILLIONS on AI infrastructure — Microsoft upped its capex 66% to $37.5B, while Amazon announced a monster $200 billion spending plan for 2026. Wall Street is asking the awkward question: "Yeah, but when do we see returns?"

It's like buying a $5,000 gaming PC but your FPS is still trash. Investors want to see the performance boost, not just the receipt.

The Rest of the Mag 7

  • Meta (META): Actually holding up okay — flat year-to-date after crushing earnings
  • Nvidia (NVDA): Also flat YTD, but all eyes are on their earnings report coming February 25
  • Apple (AAPL): Down 3.6% YTD — not great, not terrible
  • Tesla (TSLA): Down 7.3% YTD, hovering around $411
  • Alphabet (GOOGL): Down 8% over the past month

⚠️ Reality Check: When even the companies building AI infrastructure are getting punished for spending too much on AI, you know sentiment has shifted. This isn't about missing earnings — it's about fear that the AI hype cycle is getting ahead of reality.

₿ Crypto: Bitcoin Takes an L

Bitcoin holders are feeling the pain this week. BTC plunged to $64,700 in early Monday trading — down 5% in 24 hours and officially below that psychologically important $65K level.

Here's what's driving the dump:

  • Whale selling: On-chain data shows large holders ("whales") are dominating exchange inflows. The "exchange whale ratio" hit 0.64 — the highest since 2015. Translation: Big players are cashing out.
  • Recent buyers capitulating: Short-term holders were realizing losses of $1.24 billion per day at the peak of panic (Feb 6). That's cooled to ~$480M/day, but people are still selling at a loss.
  • Stablecoin inflows drying up: USDT inflows to exchanges dropped from $616M in November to just $27M. Less dry powder = less buying pressure.

The good news? This looks more like a "base-building phase" than a total meltdown. The panic selling has slowed, and the $60K-$65K range is holding as support for now. But don't expect a moonshot until we see some new catalysts.

Ethereum and alts: Getting dragged down with BTC. Altcoin deposits to exchanges are up to ~49,000/day (from 40,000 in Q4 2025), which typically signals more volatility ahead. Not a great look.

⚖️ Macro Drama: Tariffs, Courts, and Chaos

Friday delivered some wild headlines that had traders scrambling:

The Supreme Court struck down Trump's sweeping tariffs in a 6-3 ruling, calling his use of emergency powers to impose reciprocal duties illegal. Markets initially rallied on the news — Nike, Yeti, and other import-heavy stocks popped.

But then Trump announced he'd sign a new executive order imposing a 10% global tariff anyway. So... yeah. The tariff saga continues.

Gold and silver are loving the chaos — gold hit new highs, silver surged 5.6%. When uncertainty reigns, precious metals get the W.

👀 What to Watch This Week

🎯 Key Events on the Radar

Nvidia Earnings (Feb 25): This is THE event. The entire AI trade depends on whether NVDA can deliver blowout numbers and strong guidance. If they miss or guide soft, expect tech to get absolutely demolished.

  • Fed Minutes (Feb 19): Markets are parsing every word for clues on rate cuts. Current expectations: maybe 1-2 cuts in 2026, but inflation data needs to cooperate.
  • Bitcoin $65K Support: Watch if BTC can reclaim and hold $65K. If it breaks below $60K, things could get ugly fast.
  • Tariff Developments: Keep an eye on whether Trump's new 10% tariff actually materializes and how trading partners respond.
  • Retail Investor Flows: VandaTrack data shows retail investors are sitting on the sidelines — one of the weakest weeks for net flows in years. No FOMO buyers = no sustained rallies.

🎮 The Bottom Line

This market is in "risk-off" mode. Big Tech is getting repriced, crypto is consolidating, and macro uncertainty is keeping everyone cautious. It's not a crash — it's more like a prolonged boss fight where you're low on health potions and not sure when the next save point is.

For investors: If you've been waiting for a dip to buy quality names, we're getting there. Amazon at half the forward PE of Walmart? That's interesting. But don't try to catch falling knives — wait for signs of stabilization.

For crypto holders: The leverage has been flushed, panic selling is cooling, and whales are distributing. This could be a bottoming process, but don't expect V-shaped recoveries. DCA if you believe in the long-term thesis, but keep some dry powder.

For everyone: Cash is looking pretty good right now. High-yield savings at 4%+ beats losing money in a choppy market. Sometimes the best trade is no trade.

Stay sharp, stay patient, and remember: the market is a game of patience, not speed. 🎮
