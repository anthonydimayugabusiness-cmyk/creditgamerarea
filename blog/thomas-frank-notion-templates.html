How This Former Teacher Makes $50K/Month Selling Notion Templates

February 21, 2026 • 8 min read • Money From Home Series #1
C
Credit Gamer Area
Personal Finance Writer

Thomas Frank never set out to build a 7-figure business. In 2010, he was a college student struggling to stay organized. By 2024, he was making over $50,000 per month selling productivity templates on Notion — all while running a YouTube channel with 2.9 million subscribers.

His story isn't about luck or overnight success. It's about identifying a problem, building an audience, and creating products people actually want to pay for. And the best part? You can replicate his model.

$50K+ Monthly Revenue
2.9M YouTube Subscribers
14+ Years Building

Who Is Thomas Frank?

Thomas Frank started as a productivity blogger in college. While studying at Iowa State University, he created College Info Geek — a website helping students study smarter, not harder. He graduated debt-free and turned his side project into a full-time business.

For years, he made money through YouTube ad revenue, sponsorships, and affiliate marketing. But in 2021, everything changed when he discovered Notion.

"I was using Notion to organize my entire life and business. People kept asking how I set it up. So I made a video showing my system, and it went viral." — Thomas Frank

The Pivot to Product Sales

That viral video — "My Notion Setup for Productivity" — changed everything. Viewers didn't just want to see his setup; they wanted to buy it. They wanted his exact templates, pre-built and ready to use.

Thomas listened. He packaged his personal productivity system into the Ultimate Brain template — a complete life and business management system for Notion. He priced it at $99 and launched it to his email list.

💡 The Business Model

Thomas didn't just sell templates. He sold transformation. His templates promise to turn chaotic lives into organized, productive systems. People pay for the outcome, not the file.

Breaking Down the Numbers

Here's what Thomas's business looks like today:

  • Ultimate Brain template: $99 (one-time) — his flagship product
  • Creator's Companion: $149 (one-time) — for content creators
  • Notion Fundamentals course: $197 — teaches people to build their own systems
  • YouTube ad revenue: ~$15K-25K/month
  • Affiliate marketing: ~$5K-10K/month

Combined, his various income streams generate over $50,000 per month — and that's after expenses. His profit margins are incredibly high because digital products cost almost nothing to replicate.

How You Can Do This Too

Thomas's success isn't magic. It's a repeatable system:

1. Build an Audience First

Thomas spent 10 years building his YouTube channel before launching products. He gave away free value — study tips, productivity hacks, app tutorials — and built trust with his audience.

Your move: Start creating content about something you're good at. You don't need millions of followers — even 1,000 true fans can support a business.

2. Identify a Pain Point

Thomas noticed people struggled with productivity and organization. He experienced the problem himself and built a solution that worked.

Your move: What problems do you solve for friends and family? What do people ask your advice about? That's your product opportunity.

3. Create Once, Sell Forever

Digital products are the ultimate passive income. Thomas spent months building his Ultimate Brain template. Now it sells while he sleeps, with minimal ongoing work.

Your move: What digital product could you create? Templates, courses, ebooks, presets, spreadsheets — the options are endless.

4. Price for Value, Not Time

Thomas charges $99-149 for his templates. That might seem high for a digital file, but he's not selling a file — he's selling hours of saved time, reduced stress, and improved productivity.

Your move: Don't undercharge. Calculate the value your product delivers and price accordingly.

The Tools Thomas Uses

  • Notion: The platform where his products live
  • Gumroad: Payment processing and delivery (takes 10% fee)
  • ConvertKit: Email marketing to his 500K+ subscribers
  • YouTube: Free marketing and audience building
  • Loom: Creating tutorial videos for his products

Key Takeaways

Thomas Frank's story proves you don't need venture capital, a fancy degree, or even a physical product to build a 7-figure business. You need:

  1. A skill or system that solves real problems
  2. An audience that trusts your expertise
  3. The willingness to create and sell digital products
  4. Patience to build something over years, not weeks

Thomas spent 14 years building to this point. But his first dollar came from a simple blog post in college. The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step — or in this case, a single template.
