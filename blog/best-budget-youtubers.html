12 Best Budget YouTubers to Follow in 2026

Find your budgeting style and start saving more

Average Subscriber Savings

$300-800/month

Just from implementing one strategy per month

I went from saving $50/month to $800/month by binge-watching budget YouTubers and actually implementing their advice. These creators don't just talk about budgeting - they show you exactly how to do it with real numbers and real results.

🛒 Extreme Couponing & Deal Hunting

1. The Krazy Coupon Lady

@KrazyCouponLady
180K subscribers
Extreme couponing made simple

Joanie and Heather break down weekly deals at Target, CVS, Walgreens, and grocery stores. They do the math for you - just follow their matchups. Average savings: 60-80% on household items.

Best for: Busy moms who want to save on groceries without spending hours clipping coupons.

Watch on YouTube →

2. Hip2Save

@Hip2Save
95K subscribers
Daily deals & freebies

Collin finds the best daily deals, freebies, and money-saving apps. Her "What's Free Wednesday" series alone has saved me hundreds. She tests every deal before sharing.

Best for: People who love getting free stuff and stacking deals.

Watch on YouTube →

🍽️ Meal Planning & Grocery Budgeting

3. Erin Chase ($5 Dinners)

@5DollarDinners
45K subscribers
Feed family for $5/meal

Erin shows how to feed a family of 4 for $5 per meal. Her meal plans, grocery lists, and prep strategies cut my food bill in half. Real recipes, real families, real budgets.

Best for: Families spending $800+/month on groceries who want to cut it to $400.

Watch on YouTube →

4. Jordan Page (FunCheapOrFree)

@JordanPage
420K subscribers
Frugal living for big families

Jordan has 8 kids and feeds them on a shoestring budget. Her "Shelf Cooking" method (using what you have) and bulk cooking strategies are game-changers for large families.

Best for: Big families, meal preppers, and anyone overwhelmed by dinner time.

Watch on YouTube →

Minimalist vegan budgeting

Watch on YouTube →

My Personal Budget YouTube Routine

I don't watch all 12. Here's my actual system:

  1. Sunday: Watch The Krazy Coupon Lady for weekly deals
  2. Wednesday: Check Hip2Save for "What's Free Wednesday"
  3. Monthly: Watch Aja Dang's budget breakdown for motivation
  4. When stressed: Dave Ramsey debt-free screams for motivation

Pick 2-3 that match your lifestyle. Don't try to follow all 12. I'm a working mom, so I focus on family budgeting and meal planning. If you're single, you might prefer minimalism content.
