12 Underrated Destinations to Visit Before Everyone Else Does

Everyone's going to Tokyo, Paris, and Bali. But you? You're looking for something different. Here are 12 gems with real points strategies.

February 25, 2026 • 12 min read

Everyone's going to Tokyo, Paris, and Bali. But you? You're looking for something different. Places that haven't been ruined by overtourism. Destinations where you can still get a table without a reservation made three months ago.

Here are 12 underrated gems that deserve a spot on your travel list—complete with how to get there using points and miles.

🇪🇺 EUROPE

1. Ljubljana, Slovenia

Car-free center 1 hour from Lake Bled

A fairy-tale capital without the Prague crowds. Car-free city center, hilltop castle, and emerald-green rivers. Plus, you're an hour from Lake Bled.

Best for: Travelers who want European charm without the tourist crush.

💡 Points Strategy

Stay: InterContinental Ljubljana – 31,000 IHG points/night or ~$174
Fly: Fly into Zagreb (ZAG) and rent a car, or connect via Vienna. Use United miles (Star Alliance).

2. Alesund, Norway

Art Nouveau architecture Summer temps < 70°F

Art Nouveau architecture, gateway to UNESCO-protected fjords, and summer temperatures that barely hit 70°F. Perfect for escaping the heat.

💡 Points Strategy

Stay: Hotel Brosundet – 16,250 World of Hyatt points/night or ~$180
Fly: Direct flights from Oslo. Use Avios for short-haul European connections.

🇯🇵 ASIA

3. Niseko, Japan (Summer)

Summer adventure Lower prices

Everyone knows Niseko for powder skiing, but summer brings hiking, canyoning, golf, and traditional tea ceremonies—without the winter crowds or prices.

💡 Points Strategy

Stay: Hilton Niseko Village – 28,000 Hilton points/night OR Park Hyatt Niseko Hanazono – 35,000 World of Hyatt points/night
Fly: Fly to Sapporo (CTS). Use ANA miles via Virgin Atlantic for incredible value.

4. Taipei, Taiwan

Night markets English friendly

Night markets that put Bangkok's to shame, bubble tea invented here, and a fraction of the tourists you'll find in Tokyo or Seoul.

💡 Points Strategy

Stay: W Taipei or Grand Hyatt Taipei – both bookable with Hyatt points
Fly: EVA Air and Starlux fly direct from major US cities. Use Avianca LifeMiles or Air Canada Aeroplan.

🌎 THE AMERICAS

5. Bend, Oregon

22 breweries High desert

High desert landscapes, 22 breweries on the Bend Ale Trail, and the Deschutes River running through town. Crater Lake is a day trip away.

💡 Points Strategy

Stay: Newberry Hotel Bend, Tapestry Collection – 70,000 Hilton points/night
Fly: Fly to Portland (PDX), then drive 3 hours. Use Southwest points for the flight leg.

6. Mendoza, Argentina

Wine country Andes views

Argentina's wine country at the foot of the Andes. Malbec tastings, horseback riding, and thermal springs. Plus, it's winter there when it's summer here.

💡 Points Strategy

Stay: Park Hyatt Mendoza – 21,000 World of Hyatt points/night
Fly: Fly to Santiago (SCL) or Buenos Aires (EZE). Use American Airlines miles for oneworld partner awards on LATAM.

7. Halifax, Nova Scotia

Fresh seafood Cabot Trail

A reimagined waterfront, fresh seafood, and the starting point for the Cabot Trail. Canada's best-kept secret.

💡 Points Strategy

Stay: The Sutton Place Hotel Halifax – Marriott Bonvoy properties available
Fly: Direct flights from major East Coast cities. Use Air Canada Aeroplan for Star Alliance awards.

✈️ OFF THE BEATEN PATH

8. Uzbekistan

Silk Road New JW Marriott

Silk Road cities like Samarkand and Bukhara, stunning Islamic architecture, and the new JW Marriott Tashkent. Tourism infrastructure is improving fast.

💡 Points Strategy

Stay: JW Marriott Hotel Tashkent – Marriott Bonvoy points
Fly: Turkish Airlines via Istanbul (best option). Use United miles for Star Alliance awards.

🎯 The Strategy for Booking These Trips

For flights: Use Google Flights to track prices and set alerts. Book award flights 330+ days out for best availability.

For hotels: Hyatt has the best award chart for boutique properties. Hilton has the most properties in secondary cities.

For inspiration: Follow points bloggers for mistake fares and set up Scott's Cheap Flights alerts.
