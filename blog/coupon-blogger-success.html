How Two Moms Built a $30M Coupon Blog Empire (And How You Can Too)

Meet Heather Wheeler and Joanie Demer, the founders of The Krazy Coupon Lady, who turned grocery savings into a multi-million dollar business

📅 Feb 22, 2026 ⏱️ 12 min read 💰 Business Model Deep Dive

Picture this: You're a stay-at-home mom in 2009, the economy just crashed, and your family is struggling to make ends meet. You start clipping coupons to save money on groceries. Fast forward 15 years, and you've built a company with 120+ employees, millions of monthly visitors, and $30 million in annual revenue.

This isn't a fairy tale. This is the true story of Heather Wheeler and Joanie Demer, the co-founders of The Krazy Coupon Lady — one of the most successful coupon blogs in the world.

In this Money From Home Series installment, we're breaking down exactly how they did it, how much they make, and most importantly — how you can apply their strategies to build your own online income stream.

The Origin Story: From College Roommates to Business Partners

Heather Wheeler and Joanie Demer met as college roommates at BYU. Like many young adults, they graduated, started families, and found themselves navigating the challenges of household budgeting. But unlike most, they turned their money-saving hobby into a full-blown media empire.

The timing was perfect. The 2008 financial crisis had just devastated the economy. Families everywhere were looking for ways to cut costs. Couponing wasn't just trendy — it was necessary.

"We believe saving money should be free." — Heather Wheeler & Joanie Demer, Co-Founders of The Krazy Coupon Lady

That simple philosophy became their north star. While other sites charged for "exclusive" deals or premium memberships, Heather and Joanie committed to giving everything away for free. The catch? They'd make their money through advertising and affiliate partnerships — not from their readers' wallets.

The Numbers: How Much Does a Coupon Blog Really Make?

Let's talk money. According to industry data and public estimates, here's what The Krazy Coupon Lady's business looks like today:

$30.1M Annual Revenue
5.24M Monthly Visitors
124 Employees
15+ Years in Business

That's right — a blog about coupons generates over $30 million per year. But where does all that money come from?

💰 Revenue Breakdown

📢 Display Advertising (Google AdSense, Programmatic) 35-40%
🔗 Affiliate Marketing (Retail Partners) 40-45%
🤝 Brand Partnerships & Sponsored Content 10-15%
📱 App Advertising (AdMob) 5-10%

Here's the key insight: Affiliate marketing is the biggest revenue driver. Every time you click a link to Target, Walmart, CVS, or Amazon and make a purchase, The Krazy Coupon Lady earns a commission — typically 1-10% of the sale.

With millions of visitors clicking those links daily, those small percentages add up to millions of dollars annually.

The Business Model: How Coupon Blogs Make Money

Understanding the revenue streams is one thing. Understanding how to build them is another. Let's break down each income source:

1. Affiliate Marketing (The Big Money Maker)

Affiliate marketing is simple: you promote a product, someone buys through your link, and you get a cut. For coupon blogs, this works beautifully because:

  • High intent traffic: People visiting coupon sites are already planning to buy
  • Volume game: Small commissions (1-5%) on millions of transactions add up fast
  • Passive income: Old posts continue earning for years
  • Low overhead: No inventory, no shipping, no customer service

Major retailers like Amazon, Target, Walmart, and Best Buy all have affiliate programs. Commission rates vary by category — electronics might pay 1-3%, while fashion or home goods can pay 5-10%.

2. Display Advertising (The Reliable Baseline)

Display ads — those banner ads you see on websites — provide a steady baseline of income. The Krazy Coupon Lady uses Google Ad Manager and works with Freestar (a Google Certified Publishing Partner) to optimize their ad inventory.

According to Joanie Demer: "Sure, ads are just one stream among several, but it's what underwrites jobs and our bottom line. We wouldn't have 120 people working for us without it."

With 5+ million monthly visitors, even modest CPM rates (cost per thousand impressions) generate substantial revenue. At a conservative $5 CPM, that's $25,000+ per month just from display ads.

3. Brand Partnerships (The Premium Layer)

Once you have an audience, brands will pay premium rates for sponsored content, dedicated email blasts, and featured placements. A single sponsored post on a high-traffic coupon blog can command $5,000-$50,000+ depending on the scope.

The Growth Timeline: From Zero to $30M

2009

The Beginning

Heather and Joanie launch The Krazy Coupon Lady as a simple blog sharing their grocery deals. They post daily, building a small but loyal following of fellow coupon enthusiasts.

2010-2012

Early Growth

The site gains traction as the recession drives interest in frugal living. They expand beyond groceries into retail deals, pharmacy savings (CVS, Walgreens), and seasonal shopping guides.

2013-2015

Going Mobile

They launch their mobile app as smartphone usage explodes. The app sends real-time deal alerts, driving repeat engagement and creating a new advertising channel.

2016-2019

Team Expansion

What started as a two-person operation grows to dozens of employees. They hire deal hunters, editors, video creators, developers, and SEO specialists. The site becomes a full media company.

2020-2026

Empire Status

With 120+ employees and $30M+ in annual revenue, The Krazy Coupon Lady is now a major player in the affiliate marketing space. They've expanded into video content, social media, and contributor networks.

Why This Business Model Works (And Keeps Working)

Coupon blogs have staying power for several reasons:

1. Recession-Proof
When the economy struggles, more people look for deals. The Krazy Coupon Lady actually saw growth during the 2008 crash and the 2020 pandemic.

2. Evergreen Content
A "How to Coupon at CVS" guide written in 2015 still drives traffic today. Unlike news sites that need constant fresh content, coupon tutorials have long shelf lives.

3. Compounding Traffic
SEO is the secret weapon. Each new article has the potential to rank on Google and drive traffic for years. After 15 years, The Krazy Coupon Lady has thousands of ranking pages.

4. Multiple Revenue Streams
They're not dependent on any single income source. If affiliate rates drop, ad revenue picks up the slack. If ad rates fall, sponsored content can compensate.

Actionable Tips: How to Start Your Own Coupon Blog

Ready to start your own money-saving empire? Here's your roadmap:

🚀 Your 90-Day Launch Plan

  1. Pick Your Niche — Don't try to cover everything. Focus on a specific angle: grocery stores in your region, pharmacy deals, online retail, or a specific store like Target or Costco.
  2. Set Up Your Blog — Get a domain, hosting (Bluehost or SiteGround), and install WordPress. Keep it simple. You can start for under $100.
  3. Join Affiliate Programs — Apply to Amazon Associates, Target Partners, and other retail affiliate programs. Most require a live website but not huge traffic.
  4. Post Consistently — Aim for 3-5 posts per week. Document real deals you're finding. Authenticity beats perfection.
  5. Learn Basic SEO — Use free tools like Ubersuggest or Google Keyword Planner. Target long-tail keywords like "Target diaper deals this week" rather than broad terms like "coupons."
  6. Build an Email List — Offer a freebie (like a "Weekly Deal Roundup") in exchange for email addresses. This becomes your most valuable asset.
  7. Add Display Ads — Once you hit 10,000+ monthly sessions, apply to Mediavine or AdThrive. This is when ad revenue becomes meaningful.

Content Ideas That Work

  • Weekly Store Matchups: "Best Deals at CVS This Week (Feb 22-28)"
  • Beginner Guides: "How to Stack Coupons at Target: A Complete Guide"
  • Seasonal Content: "Black Friday 2026: The Best Deals Predictions"
  • Product Reviews: "Is the Costco Membership Worth It? (Real Math)"
  • Deal Roundups: "10 Freebies You Can Get This Week"

Realistic Income Expectations

Let's be honest — you won't make $30 million overnight. Here's what to expect:

  • Months 1-6: $0-$500/month (building content, learning SEO)
  • Months 6-12: $500-$2,000/month (traffic growing, first ad revenue)
  • Year 2: $2,000-$5,000/month (consistent traffic, multiple income streams)
  • Year 3+: $5,000-$20,000+/month (compounding returns, team building)

The key is consistency. Most people quit before they hit the 6-month mark. The ones who stick with it and post regularly are the ones who succeed.

The Bottom Line

Heather Wheeler and Joanie Demer proved that you don't need a revolutionary product or venture capital to build a multi-million dollar business. You need:

  • A genuine passion for helping people save money
  • Consistency in publishing content
  • Patience to let SEO and traffic compound over time
  • Smart monetization through affiliate marketing and ads

The Krazy Coupon Lady started as two moms sharing grocery deals. Today, it's a 120-person company helping millions of families stretch their budgets. The opportunity is still there — the coupon blog space is far from saturated, and new voices are always welcome.

Your journey to $100K+ starts with a single blog post. What are you waiting for?
