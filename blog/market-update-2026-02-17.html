Market Update: S&P 500 Stuck at 7K, Big Tech Bleeds, Crypto Catches Its Breath

February 17, 2026 • 7 min read

Yo, what's good traders? It's been a wild few days in the markets, and if you've been too busy grinding ranked or catching up on sleep, don't worry—I got you. Here's everything that went down from Valentine's Day through Presidents' Day weekend.

Spoiler alert: Big Tech is taking Ls, the S&P 500 is playing hard to get with that 7,000 level, and crypto is... actually being surprisingly chill? Let's dive in.

📊 The Big Picture: S&P 500 Can't Catch a Break

The S&P 500 has been flirting with the 7,000 mark like that one situationship you can't seem to escape. It touched the level earlier in the week, got rejected harder than a Bronze player in Diamond lobbies, and now we're sitting around 6,917.

The Nasdaq took an even bigger hit, dropping over 1% as tech stocks got absolutely demolished. The Dow? Yeah, it joined the pity party too, shedding over 600 points at one point this week. Not exactly the vibes we were hoping for.

What's driving this? A combo of profit-taking after the recent run-up, some nervousness about Fed policy, and honestly—Big Tech earnings didn't deliver the dopamine hit investors were craving.

💻 Big Tech: The Magnificent Seven Become the Magnificent... Meh

Remember when the "Magnificent Seven" could do no wrong? Yeah, about that...

MSFT Microsoft

Down a brutal 16.75% YTD—the worst performer of the bunch. Azure growth is slowing, and Wall Street is questioning whether that massive AI spend is actually paying off. Satya's gonna need to cook something special on the next earnings call.

AMZN Amazon

Also getting clapped with a 13.24% drop this year. AWS is still the cloud king, but growth is decelerating and retail margins are getting squeezed. That $200 billion capex announcement? Investors are side-eyeing it hard.

NVDA Nvidia

Trading around $183, basically flat over the past 6 months. The AI chip demand story is still solid (analysts have a median price target of $265), but concerns about custom AI chips from competitors and China export restrictions are keeping a lid on things. Is this a buying opportunity or a value trap? Place your bets.

TSLA Tesla

Down 6.58% this year. The Cybertruck hype is fading, competition in EVs is heating up globally, and Elon's been... well, Elon. Robotaxis are still "coming soon" (where have we heard that before?).

AAPL Apple

Relatively "okay" with a 4.50% decline. The Vision Pro hasn't exactly set the world on fire, and iPhone sales are plateauing. But hey, at least they're not Microsoft!

The takeaway? The AI trade is getting a reality check. All that capex spending (we're talking $700 billion combined from Meta, Amazon, and Google alone) needs to start showing actual returns, or investors are gonna keep rage-quitting these positions.

₿ Crypto Corner: Bitcoin Holds the Line, Ethereum Takes a Nap

While stocks were bleeding, crypto was surprisingly resilient. Here's the TL;DR:

  • Bitcoin (BTC): Holding steady around $68,000-$69,000. After dipping below $67K earlier in the week, the bulls stepped in and defended the key support level. Classic "buy the dip" behavior.
  • Ethereum (ETH): Trading near $2,050, down from recent highs but not catastrophic. Some analysts are still bullish long-term with predictions of $7K-$9K by 2030, but short-term momentum is meh.

🔥 Crypto News That Actually Matters

ETF Boom Continues: Crypto ETFs are gaining serious traction in 2026. Regulatory tailwinds are helping, with faster approval timelines and more products hitting the market. BlackRock dropped a bombshell prediction: even a 1% crypto allocation in Asia could unlock $2 trillion in new flows. That's not a typo. Trillion with a T.

Institutional Adoption: Big banks are finally getting in on the action. Morgan Stanley and other major players are rolling out crypto services to clients. The "institutional money is coming" meme is becoming reality.

Regulatory Clarity: The SEC seems to be chilling out a bit (relatively speaking). Fewer enforcement actions, more guidance, and a generally more constructive tone from regulators. Is this the beginning of crypto's "adult phase"? Maybe.

🎮 Gamer Take

If you're holding crypto, this is actually a decent time to DCA (dollar-cost average) if you believe in the long-term thesis. Bitcoin holding $68K despite stock market volatility shows there's real demand underneath. Just don't yolo your rent money—this is still crypto, and it can still nuke 50% overnight because some whale sneezed.

🌍 Macro Watch: Fed, Geopolitics, and Economic Data

Here's what's moving the macro needle:

  • Fed Policy: The Fed is in "watch and wait" mode. Inflation data has been sticky, and traders are pricing in fewer rate cuts than they were hoping for. The market wants rate cuts. The Fed is saying "not so fast." Classic standoff.
  • Geopolitical Tensions: Turmoil in Venezuela and Iran, plus tighter sanctions on Russia, are keeping oil prices elevated and adding uncertainty to the mix. Not great for risk assets.
  • Economic Data: Mixed signals overall. Some indicators show the economy is cooling, others show resilience. The "soft landing" narrative is still alive, but it's looking a bit more fragile than it did a month ago.

The Federal Reserve also released its 2026 stress test scenarios, which is basically them saying "here's how we'd break the banks if we wanted to." It's a routine exercise, but it reminds everyone that the financial system is being monitored closely.

👀 What to Watch This Week

  • More Big Tech Earnings: Keep an eye on any guidance updates. If management sounds nervous about AI ROI, expect more selling.
  • Fed Speaker Circuit: Any Fed officials dropping hints about rate cut timing will move markets instantly.
  • Crypto ETF Flows: Watch for sustained inflows into Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs. That's your signal that institutional adoption is real, not just hype.
  • S&P 500 at 7,000: Can we finally break and hold this level? Or are we looking at a deeper pullback? The next few sessions will be telling.
  • Nvidia Earnings (Next Month): This could be the catalyst that either reignites the AI trade or buries it. No pressure, Jensen.

🎯 Final Boss Thoughts

The market is in a weird spot right now. We've had a massive run-up, valuations are stretched, and the easy money from the AI hype train is getting harder to find. But the economy isn't collapsing, earnings are still decent (just not amazing), and there's plenty of liquidity sloshing around.

For the 18-30 crowd: This is actually a good time to zoom out and remember why you're investing. If you're dollar-cost averaging into index funds, keep doing your thing. If you're picking individual stocks, be selective—quality matters more in choppy markets. And if you're in crypto, make sure you're only risking what you can afford to lose completely.

The game isn't over. It's just getting harder. Time to level up your strategy.

Stay liquid, stay informed, and may your portfolios be ever green. 🎮📈
