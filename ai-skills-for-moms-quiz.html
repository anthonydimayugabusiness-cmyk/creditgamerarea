Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need technical skills or coding knowledge?

Absolutely not. Every skill and tool recommended in this quiz is designed for non-technical users. If you can use a smartphone and basic apps like email, you have all the technical skills you need.

How much do these AI tools cost?

Most of the tools I recommend have free tiers that are sufficient to get started. For the few that require payment, I always suggest starting with free alternatives first. You shouldn't need to spend more than $20/month on AI tools to see real results.

How long until I see results?

For time-saving skills, you'll see benefits immediately - often after your first use. For income-generating skills, most people start earning within 2-4 weeks of consistent effort, though this varies based on the skill and time invested.

What if I don't have much free time?

This quiz specifically accounts for limited time. Many of the recommended skills can be done in 15-30 minute chunks, and some (like AI meal planning) actually create time rather than consume it.
