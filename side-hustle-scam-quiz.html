Side Hustle Quiz: Can You Spot the Scam? 🕵️‍♀️

You play Design Home, The Sims, or earn on JustPlay for extra cash. But do you know which side hustles are legit? Test your scam radar!

Question 1 of 10

You see an ad: "Earn $500/day playing games! Just download our app and watch ads." What's the red flag? 🚩

Correct! 🎯

Real reward apps (like JustPlay, Mistplay) pay $1-5/day, not $500. If it sounds too good to be true, it is. Scams use unrealistic earnings to get your personal info or make you pay upfront fees.

Question 2 of 10

You're cashing out $25 from a survey app. Which payout method is SAFEST? 💳

Correct! 🎯

PayPal and major gift cards (Amazon, Target) are the safest payout methods. Never give your bank account details or home address to survey apps. Crypto payouts from unknown apps are often scams.

Question 3 of 10

A "design app" asks you to pay $19.99 upfront to "unlock premium earning features." What do you do? 🎨

Correct! 🎯

Legit reward apps (Design Home, JustPlay, Mistplay) are FREE. Any app requiring upfront payment to "earn" is a scam. Real apps make money from ads, not from you.

Question 4 of 10

You earned $50 on a reward app. How much should you set aside for taxes? 💰

Correct! 🎯

ALL income is taxable, even under $600. The $600 threshold is just when apps send you a 1099 form. Set aside 15-30% based on your tax bracket. Working moms especially - this affects your total household income!

Question 5 of 10

Which of these is a REAL way to earn from gaming apps? 🎮

Correct! 🎯

Real apps (JustPlay, Mistplay, Swagbucks) let you redeem points for gift cards or PayPal. Never pay to access earnings, never send money to "traders," and watch out for MLM/pyramid structures disguised as gaming apps.

Question 6 of 10

You're a working mom earning $200/month on reward apps. What's the smartest financial move? 👩‍👧‍👦

Correct! 🎯

$200/month = $2,400/year! That's a family vacation fund, emergency savings, or investment seed money. Working moms know every dollar counts. Make your side hustle work FOR your family's future.

Question 7 of 10

An app asks for your Social Security Number to "verify your identity" for a $5 signup bonus. You should: 🔒

Correct! 🎯

NEVER give your SSN to gaming/reward apps. Identity theft is real and devastating. Legit apps pay through PayPal or gift cards without needing your SSN upfront. Only established platforms (like Swagbucks after high earnings) might need tax info.

Question 8 of 10

What's the biggest time waster in "beer money" apps? ⏰

Correct! 🎯

Apps with $50+ minimum cashouts are designed so you never actually get paid. Good apps (JustPlay, Mistplay) let you cash out at $1-5. Working moms don't have time for games that don't pay. Check cashout minimums BEFORE you start!

Question 9 of 10

You see a Facebook group: "Moms Making $1000/Month Playing Games!" The admin DMs you to join. What's the scam? 👩‍💻

Correct! 🎯

These are MLM/pyramid schemes targeting working moms. They sell "courses" or require you to recruit other moms. Real reward apps don't need private groups or recruitment. If the money comes from recruiting, not from actual app usage, it's a scam.

Question 10 of 10

Best way to track your reward app earnings for taxes? 📊

Correct! 🎯

Screenshot every cashout with date and amount. Use a simple spreadsheet (Google Sheets is free). Working moms are busy - spend 5 minutes monthly, save hours at tax time. Apps often don't send 1099s under $600, but YOU still owe taxes on that income.
