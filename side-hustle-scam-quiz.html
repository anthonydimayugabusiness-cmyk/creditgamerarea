Protecting Yourself from Side Hustle Scams: A Working Mom's Guide

Reading time: 6 minutes | Quiz: 10 questions

Advertisement

You're a working mom who unwinds with Design Home during lunch breaks. Or maybe you play The Sims after the kids go to bed. And somewhere along the way, you discovered reward apps like JustPlay or Mistplay that actually pay you for playing games.

But for every legitimate app, there are ten scams waiting to steal your time, personal information, or worse—your money.

The "Too Good To Be True" Trap

Here's the reality check: Legitimate reward apps pay $1-5 per day. That's $30-150 per month—not life-changing money, but real money for real effort. Scams promise $500+ per day for minimal work.

Advertisement

Real apps make money through advertising and data (with your consent). They share a small portion with you. Scams make money by charging you upfront fees or selling your personal information.

Red Flags Every Working Mom Should Know

You don't have time to research every app thoroughly. That's why you need a mental checklist:

  • Requires payment to start earning? Red flag.
  • Promises unrealistic earnings? Red flag.
  • Asks for your Social Security Number? Major red flag.
  • Private Facebook groups requiring recruitment? Pyramid scheme.
Advertisement

Protecting Your Personal Information

As a working mom, your identity is valuable. Never provide your Social Security Number, bank account details, or home address to gaming or survey apps. Legitimate platforms pay through PayPal or gift cards.

Create a dedicated email address for reward apps. Use a unique password. These simple steps create barriers that stop most identity theft attempts.

The Tax Reality Nobody Talks About

That $50 you earned is taxable income. Even if it's under $600 and you don't get a 1099, you still owe taxes on it.

Set aside 20% of every payout for taxes. Screenshot your earnings. Keep a simple spreadsheet. These habits take five minutes per month but save hours at tax time.
