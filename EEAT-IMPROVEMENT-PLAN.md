# E-E-A-T Improvement Plan for Credit Gamer Area

## Current Status: Needs Work for AdSense/YMYL

Finance is a YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) topic. Google and AdSense require strong E-E-A-T:
- **Experience** - First-hand knowledge
- **Expertise** - Credentials/qualifications  
- **Authoritativeness** - Recognition in field
- **Trustworthiness** - Transparency, accuracy, security

---

## Immediate Actions Needed

### 1. Author Pages (HIGH PRIORITY)

Create `/about.html` with:
- **Real author name and photo** (not just "Credit Gamer Area")
- **Credentials**: Any finance background, certifications, degrees
- **Experience**: Why you're qualified to write about money
- **Personal story**: How you learned this stuff
- **Contact info**: Email, social links

**If you don't have finance credentials:**
- Be transparent: "I'm not a financial advisor, but I've [relevant experience]"
- Cite sources: Link to government sites, financial institutions
- Show research: "Based on 50+ hours researching..."

### 2. Add Author Bylines to Every Article

Current: "Credit Gamer Area"

Better options:
- "By [Your Name] | Personal Finance Writer"
- "Reviewed by [Name], [Credential]"
- "Based on [X] years of [relevant experience]"

### 3. Disclaimer Improvements

Current disclaimers are good but need to be MORE prominent:

Add to EVERY article (top of page, not just bottom):
```
⚠️ Disclaimer: This content is for educational purposes only. 
I am not a financial advisor. Always consult a qualified professional 
before making financial decisions.
```

### 4. About Page Must Include

- **Who you are** (real name, photo)
- **Why you started this site**
- **Your qualifications** (even if informal)
- **Editorial standards** (how you fact-check)
- **How you make money** (affiliate disclosure)
- **Contact information**
- **Last updated date**

### 5. Cite Authoritative Sources

Link to:
- **Government**: IRS.gov, CFPB.gov, FTC.gov
- **Financial institutions**: Federal Reserve, FDIC
- **Data sources**: BLS.gov, Census.gov
- **Studies**: Pew Research, academic papers

Example:
> "According to the [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau](link), 45% of Americans..."

### 6. Update Existing Content

Add to each article:
- [ ] Author byline with credentials
- [ ] "Last updated" date
- [ ] Sources cited section
- [ ] More prominent disclaimer
- [ ] Author photo/bio at bottom

### 7. Trust Signals to Add

- **HTTPS** ✅ (already done)
- **Privacy policy** ✅ (already done)
- **Terms of service** ✅ (already done)
- **Contact page** with real email
- **Social media presence** (even if small)
- **Physical address** (if comfortable)

### 8. Content Quality Markers

Google looks for:
- **Original research** - Your own data/surveys
- **First-hand experience** - "I tried this..."
- **Comprehensive coverage** - Thorough, not thin content
- **Regular updates** - Fresh dates
- **No spelling/grammar errors**
