Frequently Asked Questions

Are your quizzes really free?

Yes! All our quizzes are 100% free. No sign-up required, no credit card needed, no premium tiers. We believe financial education should be accessible to everyone.

Can I suggest a quiz topic?

Absolutely! We love hearing from our community. Use the contact form above or tweet at us with your suggestions. We're always looking for new topics to cover.

How accurate is your information?

We work with financial experts to ensure our content is accurate and up-to-date. However, we're not financial advisors. Always do your own research or consult a professional for major financial decisions.

Do you have a mobile app?

Not yet! Our website is fully mobile-responsive though, so you can take quizzes on your phone browser. An app is on our roadmap for the future.
