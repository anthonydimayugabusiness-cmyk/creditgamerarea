# Domain Agency Business Structure - Multi-Site Portfolio

## 🏢 RECOMMENDED STRUCTURE: Parent LLC + DBA/Fictitious Names

### Option 1: Single LLC with Multiple DBAs (Recommended)

```
[Your Agency Name] LLC (Parent)
    ├── DBA: Credit Gamer Area
    ├── DBA: Future Site 2
    ├── DBA: Future Site 3
    └── DBA: Client Sites (if offering services)
```

**Why this works:**
- One tax return, one EIN, one bank account
- Clean separation for branding/marketing
- Easy to add new sites
- Professional when dealing with advertisers

---

## 📋 STEP-BY-STEP SETUP

### Step 1: Form the Parent LLC

**Name ideas:**
- Dimayuga Digital LLC
- Domain Agency LLC
- [Your City] Media Group LLC
- Niche Ventures LLC
- Content Holdings LLC

**Formation:**
- File with your state (Delaware, Wyoming, or your home state)
- Cost: $50-$500 depending on state
- Get EIN from IRS (free)
- Open business bank account

### Step 2: Register DBAs ("Doing Business As")

For each site like Credit Gamer Area:

**Option A: State-level DBA**
- File with your state's Secretary of State
- Cost: $10-$100 per DBA
- Required if using a different name than LLC

**Option B: County-level DBA**
- Some states require county filing
- Check local requirements

**Option C: No formal DBA (if using domain as brand only)**
- Just operate CreditGamerArea.com under the LLC
- Use "Credit Gamer Area is a [Parent LLC] company" in footer
- Simpler, but less formal separation

### Step 3: Domain Ownership Structure

**Recommended:**
```
Owner: [Your Agency] LLC
Registrant: [Your Agency] LLC
Admin: Your personal email (for control)
```

**WHOIS Privacy:** Always enabled

## 💰 FINANCIAL STRUCTURE

### One Business Bank Account (Simpler)

**Account:** [Your Agency] LLC - Operating Account

**Tracking by Site:**
Use accounting software (Wave/QuickBooks) to tag transactions:
- Revenue: Credit Gamer Area | Site 2 | Site 3
- Expenses: Credit Gamer Area Ads | Site 2 Ads

**Benefits:**
- One account to manage
- Easy to see portfolio performance
- Simpler taxes

### Multiple Accounts (More Complex)

Only if sites get HUGE ($10K+/month each):
- [Agency] LLC - Main Account
- [Agency] LLC - Credit Gamer Area (sub-account)
- [Agency] LLC - Site 2 (sub-account)

**Most banks offer free sub-accounts:**
- Mercury: Multiple accounts free
- Relay: Up to 20 accounts free

## 📊 TAX STRUCTURE

### Default: LLC Taxed as Disregarded Entity

**How it works:**
- All income/expenses flow to your personal tax return
- Schedule C (if sole owner)
- One tax return for all sites

**Example:**
```
Credit Gamer Area revenue:     +$2,000
Site 2 revenue:                +$500
Site 3 revenue:                +$0
Total revenue:                 $2,500

Expenses (ads, hosting):       -$1,000
Net profit:                    $1,500 (taxed as personal income)
```

### Upgrade: LLC Taxed as S-Corp (Later)

**When:** Making $50K+/year consistently
**Benefit:** Save ~15% on self-employment tax
**Cost:** Payroll service (~$50/month)

**One of each:**

- Ad accounts (can use one Business Manager with multiple ad accounts)

## 📋 ADSENSE SETUP FOR MULTI-SITE

### One AdSense Account (Recommended)

Google allows multiple sites under one publisher account:

```
AdSense Account: [Your Agency]
├── Site: creditgamerarea.com
├── Site: futuresite2.com
└── Site: futuresite3.com
```

**Benefits:**
- One payment threshold ($100)
- Combined earnings
- Easier management
- One tax form (1099)

### Alternative: Separate AdSense Accounts

Only if:
- One site gets banned (doesn't affect others)
- Selling individual sites later
- Different business partners per site

**Downside:** Each needs $100 minimum payout
