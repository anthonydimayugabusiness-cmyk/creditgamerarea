🔧 Setup Guide

Step-by-step instructions for all your tasks

Cloudflare reCAPTCHA Affiliates Traffic
HIGH PRIORITY - BLOCKS ADSENSE

1. Sign Up for Cloudflare

Cloudflare protects your site from bots, DDoS attacks, and invalid traffic that could get your AdSense banned.

Why this matters: AdSense will ban you if you get invalid traffic (bots clicking ads). Cloudflare blocks 90%+ of bot traffic before it reaches your site.
1

Create Account

Go to Cloudflare and sign up for a free account.

Sign Up for Cloudflare →
2

Add Your Domain

Click "Add a Site" and enter: creditgamerarea.com

Select the Free Plan (it's enough for our needs)

3

Update Nameservers

Cloudflare will give you 2 nameservers. You need to update these at your domain registrar (where you bought creditgamerarea.com).

Example nameservers (yours will be different):
lara.ns.cloudflare.com
greg.ns.cloudflare.com

Where to update: Log into your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google Domains, etc.) → Find DNS settings → Replace existing nameservers with Cloudflare's.

4

Send Me Your Cloudflare Details

Once setup is complete, send me:

  • Your Cloudflare account email
  • Zone ID (found on the right sidebar in Cloudflare dashboard)
  • Global API Key (My Profile → API Tokens → Global API Key)
I'll then: Configure bot protection, set up firewall rules, and enable all security features.
HIGH PRIORITY - BLOCKS INVALID TRAFFIC

2. Sign Up for reCAPTCHA v3

Google's reCAPTCHA detects bots on your forms and quizzes. Essential for AdSense protection.

1

Get Google Account Ready

Make sure you're logged into the Google account you want to use for this.

2

Register Your Site

Go to the reCAPTCHA admin console and register your domain.

Register for reCAPTCHA →
3

Configure Settings

When registering:

  • Label: Credit Gamer Area
  • Type: reCAPTCHA v3
  • Domains: creditgamerarea.com, www.creditgamerarea.com
  • Owners: Your email
4

Get Your Keys

After registration, you'll get two keys. Send me both:

Site Key (starts with numbers/letters, ~40 chars)

Secret Key (starts with numbers/letters, ~40 chars)
I'll then: Install reCAPTCHA on all quiz forms and contact pages.
