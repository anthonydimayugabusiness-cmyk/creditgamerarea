Side Hustle Scams - Question 1 of 10
Question 1 of 10
You see an ad: "Earn $500/day playing games! Just download our app and watch ads." What's the red flag? 🚩


Real reward apps pay $1-5/day, not $500. If it sounds too good to be true, it is. Scams use unrealistic earnings to get your personal info.
