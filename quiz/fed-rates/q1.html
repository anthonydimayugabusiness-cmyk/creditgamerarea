What is the Federal Reserve's main tool for influencing the economy?

The federal funds rate is the interest rate banks charge each other for overnight loans. It's the Fed's primary tool for monetary policy. Changes to this rate ripple through the economy, affecting credit cards, mortgages, savings accounts, and more.
